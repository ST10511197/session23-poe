Our Story

About Session 23

The people, the sound, and the reason we do this.

Party With The English

Session 23 started as a small circle of friends across Gqeberha and Johannesburg who wanted parties that actually respected the music. What began as informal get-togethers grew into a full events brand built around Amapiano, House and Hip-Hop — genres that define the sound of a generation.

Every Session is built the same way: a tight lineup, a room that's designed for the crowd rather than the other way around, and a black-and-gold identity that's become recognisable across both cities.

What We Stand For

Good music, well-run doors, and a crowd that comes back. No shortcuts on sound or safety.

Mission & Vision

Where We're Headed

Mission

To create Amapiano, House and Hip-Hop events that put the crowd and the music first, across Gqeberha and Johannesburg.

Vision

To grow Session 23 into a recognised events and brand-partnership name across South Africa's major cities.

Values

Quality over quantity, consistency in the black-and-gold identity, and real relationships with our crowd and partners.
